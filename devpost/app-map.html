Beats Bazaar App Map

Architectural blueprint and guided reference for the Global Beat Marketplace, 24/7 Freestyle Cypher Room, and Gemini 3.8 Flash AI Cypher DJ.

Platform Architecture & Core Pillars

Beats Bazaar is built around two complementary creator experiences supported by instant monetization and artificial intelligence:

  • Marketplace Surface (src/components/MarketplaceView.tsx): Catalog discovery for recording artists to audition protected previews, search across dynamic genres, explore verified producer storefronts, and license commercial rights.
  • Freestyle Cypher Deck (src/components/CypherRoomView.tsx): Continuous, auto-advancing audio stream designed for live MC cyphers, jam sessions, and campus battles, featuring custom crossfade duration, waveform visualizer, and instant producer tipping.
  • AI Cypher DJ (src/components/AiDjModal.tsx & src/app/api/ai-dj/route.ts): Natural-language vibe interpreter powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash that translates plain English moods into matching catalog tracks with real-time DJ commentary.
  • Monetization & Licensing Engine (src/components/BachsModal.tsx & LicenseCertificateModal.tsx): Dual fiat and crypto checkout delivering verified digital License Certificates and untagged master WAV downloads.

Core User Journey Flow

Reference Code Route

Stop 1: Audio Orchestration & Playback Engine

src/context/AudioContext.tsx (anchor: AudioProvider)

Holds the central player state, dynamic audio element crossfade interpolation, track advancement timers, and catalog persistence in localStorage.

Stop 2: AI Cypher DJ & Gemini 3.8 Flash Integration

src/app/api/ai-dj/route.ts (anchor: POST)

Invokes Google GenAI SDK with model gemini-3.8-flash to interpret user prompts against the beat metadata catalog, equipped with an automatic zero-downtime semantic fallback.

Stop 3: Verified Digital License Generation

src/app/page.tsx (anchor: handleBachsSuccess)

Assembles immutable digital legal certificates upon checkout verification, calculates exclusive buyout rights, and unlocks untagged master downloads.

To Change This, Start Here

  • Add or update seeded music catalog: Edit src/data/seedBeats.ts to add new producers, audio files, tempos, or tags.
  • Modify crossfade curves or transitions: Edit src/context/AudioContext.tsx inside the fadeTimer handlers.
  • Adjust Gemini AI prompts or JSON output structure: Edit src/app/api/ai-dj/route.ts inside systemInstruction.
  • Customize commercial legal license terms: Edit terms definitions in src/app/page.tsx.

A Practice to Reuse: Resilient Model Fallbacks

Pattern: Graceful Degradation in Agentic / LLM Integrations.

In src/app/api/ai-dj/route.ts, live calls to gemini-3.8-flash are wrapped with a deterministic local semantic keyword & genre scoring fallback. When an API key is missing or network rate-limits occur during testing or hackathon judging, the platform seamlessly returns an intelligent recommendation without crashing the UI or returning a 500 error.

Reusability: Always design consumer AI features with a deterministic, local heuristic layer. This guarantees a smooth user experience across sandbox, offline, or demo environments.
